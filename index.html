How We Build
From Vision to Delivery — Our Development Process
🎯
Step 1
Direction & Strategy
Dustin works closely with the team to establish the product concept — identifying business needs, user flows, and strategic direction so everyone is aligned before work begins.
›
⚡
Step 2
Initial Prototype
Dustin oversees and contributes to early-stage prototypes to give the team a clear, concrete starting point — enough to communicate the vision, then the team takes it from there.
›
🤝
Step 3
Team Takes Ownership
The engineering team takes full ownership of the build — driving architecture, development, and refinement. Dustin stays close to answer questions and align on intent, but the team leads the way.
›
✅
Step 4
Polished Delivery
The team brings the product as close to ready as reasonably possible, then opens it up — inviting C-Suite, Operations, Sales, and Marketing to review, contribute, and shape it before anything goes live. Great products take input from the whole company.
